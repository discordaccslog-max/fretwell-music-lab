Seventh Chord Essentials

The five seventh chords: spelled, built in every inversion, found on the fretboard and heard.

About this course

Seventh chords are introduced in pairs that are easy to mix up, then all five together and from any root. You build them on the keyboard and fretboard, identify them by ear, and finish by spelling them with jazz chord symbols.

  • 10 lessons, plus 3 bonus lessons with the bonus add-on
  • Level: Intermediate
  • Each lesson has a set number of questions and a pass mark; repeat any lesson as often as you like
  • Your lesson results are saved in your browser and can be moved to another device with a backup code
