Pentatonic & Blues Path

The pentatonic and blues scales, spelled, played and heard, in eight lessons.

About this course

You build the minor and major pentatonic scales and both blues scales from different roots, find their box shapes on the fretboard, and learn to tell them apart by ear and by scale degree.

  • 8 lessons, plus 3 bonus lessons with the bonus add-on
  • Level: Beginner
  • Each lesson has a set number of questions and a pass mark; repeat any lesson as often as you like
  • Your lesson results are saved in your browser and can be moved to another device with a backup code

Who it’s for

  • Guitarists and keyboard players starting to improvise
  • Players who want the blues scale in more than one key

Who it isn’t for

  • Players who already use these scales in every key

What you need

  • A modern web browser on a computer, tablet or phone
  • Speakers or headphones
  • The ability to hear audio — this course includes ear training that depends on listening

About the sound: all instrument sounds are synthesized in your browser. They are not recordings of real instruments.
