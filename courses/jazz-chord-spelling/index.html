Jazz Chord Spelling Drills

Spell every jazz chord symbol, from seventh chords to thirteenths and altered dominants.

About this course

One chord family per lesson: sevenths, sixths, ninths, elevenths, thirteenths and altered dominants, first from natural roots and then from any root. The last lessons mix families together.

  • 10 lessons, plus 3 bonus lessons with the bonus add-on
  • Level: Intermediate
  • Each lesson has a set number of questions and a pass mark; repeat any lesson as often as you like
  • Your lesson results are saved in your browser and can be moved to another device with a backup code

Who it’s for

  • Players reading jazz lead sheets
  • Arrangers who want chord symbols to be automatic

Who it isn’t for

  • Players who do not yet know seventh chords (start with Seventh Chord Essentials)

What you need

  • A modern web browser on a computer, tablet or phone
  • Speakers or headphones
  • The ability to hear audio — this course includes ear training that depends on listening

About the sound: all instrument sounds are synthesized in your browser. They are not recordings of real instruments.
