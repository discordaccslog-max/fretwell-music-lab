Foundations

Foundations Bundle

All five Foundations tools, unlocked with a single key.

What it trains

The Foundations Bundle includes Note Reader, Interval Ear Trainer, Scale & Mode Explorer, Chord Builder & Ear Trainer and Rhythm Lab. One license key opens all five in your practice room, and every tool keeps its own progress, settings and practice sets.

  • Reading notes on the staff, fretboard and keyboard
  • Hearing and naming intervals
  • Spelling, seeing and hearing scales and modes in every key
  • Building and hearing triads and seventh chords in every inversion
  • Reading and tapping rhythms with timing feedback

Included tools

  • Note Reader: Staff & Fretboard Trainer

    Name notes on the staff, the fretboard and the keyboard until reading them is automatic.

    Exercises: Staff notes · Fretboard notes · Find it on the string · Keyboard notes · Staff to keyboard

  • Interval Ear Trainer

    Hear the distance between two notes and name it — ascending, descending or together.

    Exercises: Ascending intervals · Descending intervals · Harmonic intervals · Mixed · Compare (reference mode)

  • Scale & Mode Explorer

    Every major, minor, pentatonic, blues and modal scale in every key — seen, heard and built from memory.

    Exercises: Build the scale · Scale by ear · Scale degrees · Fretboard patterns

  • Chord Builder & Ear Trainer

    Build triads and seventh chords in every inversion, then learn to hear their quality and bass note.

    Exercises: Build on the keyboard · Build on the fretboard · Spell the chord · Quality by ear · Inversion by ear

  • Rhythm Lab

    Read and tap rhythms against a metronome, with timing feedback on every single tap.

    Exercises: Read and tap · Steady pulse · Rhythm by ear

Each tool’s page lists every exercise and setting, with a demo.

Try it

A short interactive demo built from the same code as the real tool.

Instruments and clefs

Treble and bass clef, guitar fretboard (six tunings, left-handed option), piano keyboard, and rhythm notation.

Who it’s for

  • Players building a complete foundation in theory and ear training
  • Anyone who would otherwise buy three or more of the Foundations tools

Who it isn’t for

  • Players who only need one area — each tool is also sold on its own
  • Anyone wanting the Studio Editions, which are separate products

What you need

  • A modern web browser on a computer, tablet or phone
  • Speakers or headphones
  • The ability to hear audio — this tool includes ear training that depends on listening

About the sound: all instrument sounds are synthesized in your browser. They are not recordings of real instruments.

In every tool

  • Instant feedback on every answer, with ✓ and ✗ marks and the correct answer shown.
  • Settings that save themselves, plus named practice sets you can switch between.
  • A session summary when you finish, with your accuracy and the items to review.
  • A reference view for looking things up without being quizzed.
  • Progress and streaks: accuracy over time and your weakest items, stored only on your device.
  • Progress backup: move your progress to another device with a backup code or file.
  • Works offline once unlocked, and installs to your home screen or desktop.
  • Keyboard shortcuts, visible focus, a larger-interface setting, and dark mode.
