Foundations

Chord Builder & Ear Trainer

Build triads and seventh chords in every inversion, then learn to hear their quality and bass note.

What it trains

Chord Builder & Ear Trainer covers the chords at the centre of most music: major, minor, diminished and augmented triads, and major 7th, dominant 7th, minor 7th, half-diminished 7th and diminished 7th chords, in every inversion. You build them on the keyboard and fretboard, spell them, and identify them by ear.

  • Building any triad or seventh chord on the keyboard and on the fretboard
  • Spelling chords correctly, including double flats in diminished 7ths
  • Hearing chord quality (major, minor, diminished, augmented and the five seventh-chord types)
  • Hearing which chord tone is in the bass: root position, 1st, 2nd or 3rd inversion

Exercises

Build on the keyboard
Press the keys of the chord in the inversion shown. The lowest note must be the right chord tone.
Build on the fretboard
Tap one note per string to make the chord in the inversion shown.
Spell the chord
Enter the chord tones from the root up with correct spelling.
Quality by ear
Hear a chord and name its quality.
Inversion by ear
Hear a chord and name its inversion.

Try it

A short interactive demo built from the same code as the real tool.

Settings you can change

  • Chord types included (any combination of the four triads and five seventh chords)
  • Inversions included
  • Root difficulty: natural roots only, or roots with sharps and flats
  • Ear training playback: block chord, broken chord first, or both
  • Tone (keyboard or guitar), tuning and left-handed fretboard
  • Session length

Instruments and clefs

Piano keyboard, guitar fretboard (six tunings, left-handed option), and treble and bass staff.

Reference view

The chord dictionary shows every triad and seventh chord on any root and in any inversion, on staff, keyboard and fretboard, with block and broken playback.

Who it’s for

  • Guitarists and keyboard players who want to understand the chords they play
  • Theory students learning inversions and seventh chords
  • Anyone who wants to hear the difference between chord qualities

Who it isn’t for

  • Players looking for a chord-shape dictionary to strum along with songs
  • Jazz players after extended and altered chords (those are in the Jazz Harmony Studio)

What you need

  • A modern web browser on a computer, tablet or phone
  • Speakers or headphones
  • The ability to hear audio — this tool includes ear training that depends on listening

About the sound: all instrument sounds are synthesized in your browser. They are not recordings of real instruments.
