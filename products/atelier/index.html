Studio Edition

Atelier Edition: Advanced Aural Skills

The most demanding training in the catalogue: compound intervals, seventh-chord inversions, applied dominants, modulations, two-voice and rhythmic dictation.

What it trains

The Atelier Edition gathers the hardest listening work into one standalone product. It assumes you are comfortable with intervals, triads and simple dictation, and goes further: intervals wider than an octave, every seventh chord in every inversion, secondary dominants and modulations inside real progressions, two-voice dictation and rhythms in compound and irregular meters. Custom practice sets and detailed progress tracking show exactly where to focus.

  • Compound intervals from the minor 9th to two octaves
  • All five seventh-chord qualities in all four positions, by ear
  • Secondary (applied) dominants and modulations to closely related keys, heard inside progressions
  • Two-voice dictation and rhythmic dictation in 5/8, 6/8, 7/8, 9/8, 12/8 and 5/4

Exercises

Compound intervals
Name intervals from the minor 9th to two octaves, played up, down or together.
Seventh chords by ear
Name the quality and the inversion of a seventh chord.
Secondary dominants
Hear a progression and name the applied dominant it contains: V⁷/ii, V⁷/iii, V⁷/IV, V⁷/V or V⁷/vi.
Modulations
Hear a progression and say whether it stays home or moves to the dominant, subdominant, relative minor or supertonic minor.
Two-voice dictation
Hear two voices moving note against note and write both on the grand staff.
Rhythmic dictation
Hear a rhythm in a compound or irregular meter and write it.

What makes it a Studio Edition

Compared with the Foundations tools, this edition asks more of you in concrete ways:

Harder material
Intervals beyond the octave, seventh chords in every inversion, and harmony heard in context rather than as isolated chords.
Multi-voice and rhythmic dictation
You write two independent voices at once, and rhythms in meters such as 7/8 and 12/8.
Detailed progress tracking
Accuracy over time for each exercise, your weakest items, and the answers you most often confuse with each other.

Who it’s for

  • Advanced students who find the Foundations ear training comfortable
  • Players who want to follow harmony and rhythm in more complex music by ear

Who it isn’t for

  • Beginners — start with the Interval Ear Trainer and Chord Builder
  • Anyone who cannot hear audio — every exercise is aural

What you need

  • A modern web browser on a computer, tablet or phone
  • Speakers or headphones
  • The ability to hear audio — this tool includes ear training that depends on listening

About the sound: all instrument sounds are synthesized in your browser. They are not recordings of real instruments.

  • A reference view for looking things up without being quizzed.
