Questions

Read all questions

How do license keys work?

When you buy a tool, the order confirmation page shows your license key straight away. It looks like FRET-ET-7KQ4M-X9D2P-H6TR. Enter it on the unlock page and the tool opens in your practice room. The key is checked in your browser — there is no account, password or sign-in.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Enter the same key on each of your devices — your computer, tablet and phone. Each device remembers the key after you enter it once. Keys are for your own personal use, so please don’t share them.

Are the sounds recordings of real instruments?

No. Every sound — the plucked guitar tone, the keyboard tone and the metronome click — is synthesized in your browser as you play. They are designed to sound pleasant and clear, but they are not recordings of real instruments.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once you have unlocked your tools and opened the practice room while online, it is stored on your device and keeps working without an internet connection. You can also install it to your home screen or desktop like an app, using your browser’s “Install” or “Add to Home Screen” option.
